The road to the platinum

  1. Stage 1 — Story first. Play the chapters and the named story missions. Do the two partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) the first time a partner is free-roaming with you. Save before the final mission.
  2. Stage 2 — Side contacts. Finish every contact strand (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), Bret's hunt, and 8 stranger strands. Answer every phone call; talk to every question-mark stranger you pass.
  3. Stage 3 — World & collectibles. Explore all 52 areas, meditate at 18 spots, WAiNK 100 locations, finish 3 card sets, discover 100 vehicles, buy 8 garages, register 10 vehicles, and enjoy the beach cocktail and the showroom smash.
  4. Stage 4 — Activities & crime. Grind the counters: 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone races, 20 race wins, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults, 9 gang operations, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 500 choices.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & platinum. Open the in-game 100% checklist, clear what is left, earn A Long Road, and the platinum pops.

Nothing here needs a second playthrough if you save before the finale and do the missable-flagged trophies on time. Missable trophies are marked with a warning tag.
